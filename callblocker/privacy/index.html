1. Information the app uses

We (RomanXView, the developer) do not run any server for this app and do not receive the information below. It is processed and stored on your device only, unless you have turned on Android backup (see section 6).

Incoming call numbers

When a call comes in, Android gives the app the caller's number so it can compare it with your blocked prefixes. This check happens instantly on your phone. If a call is blocked, the number, the matching prefix and the time are saved in your on-device call history. Numbers of calls that are not blocked are not saved, except that, when you turn on "Block spam calls", numbers that are not in your contacts are kept on your phone for 30 minutes to spot waves of calls from one number series, then deleted. Android gives call screening apps the calls of people saved in your contacts only if you allow Contacts access. Saved contacts are never blocked.

Prefixes, settings and schedules

The prefixes you add, your theme, language, blocking and pop-up settings, your quiet hours schedule and the numbers you allow to ring are stored on your phone.

Your profile

The app asks for your name. Email address and profile photo are optional. The photo is chosen with Android's photo picker and a resized copy is kept privately inside the app. Your profile is only shown inside the app and is never sent to us.

SIM details (optional)

If you allow phone access, the app reads your SIM's carrier name, country and, where your carrier provides it, your phone number, to show them on your profile. They are read when you open the profile and are not uploaded.

Contacts

Contacts access is optional. The app asks for it when you add numbers that may ring during quiet hours, because Android only reports calls from saved contacts to the app after you allow it. With this access the app checks, on your phone, whether a calling number is saved in your contacts, so that your chosen numbers can ring and saved contacts are never blocked. Your contacts are not copied, uploaded or shared. "Pick contact" saves only the name and number you select. The options for starred contacts, all contacts and repeat callers use Android's Do Not Disturb settings. You can refuse or withdraw this access in Android settings.

3. Advertising

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob on the Home and Calls screens. To serve, personalise (where allowed) and measure these ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version and app version,
  • ad interactions, such as views and taps, and diagnostic data about ad loading.

This collection is done by Google, under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Your blocked prefixes, call history, profile and contacts are never shared with Google or any ad partner.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with anyone. The only data that leaves your device is what the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google, described in section 3. The app contains no analytics or tracking of its own.

5. Security

Your information is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Release versions of the app do not write phone numbers or prefixes to the system log.

6. Storage, backup and deletion

  • Your data stays on your phone for as long as the app is installed.
  • You can delete your blocked call history at any time in Settings → Clear history, remove prefixes and allowed numbers in the app, and edit or remove your profile details and photo.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is controlled by you in your phone's backup settings and handled by Google.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
